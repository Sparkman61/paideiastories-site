How it works
Paideia Stories generates a unique, illustrated bedtime story personalized to your child every night — and delivers it directly to your email inbox at the time you choose. No app to open. No subscription box to manage. Just a story waiting for you at bedtime, ready to read aloud.
When you set up your child's profile, you tell us their name, age, appearance (hair, eyes, skin tone), interests, any themes to avoid, and their educational focus. Every story is generated fresh using this profile — your child appears as the central character, the story reflects their interests, and the vocabulary and complexity match their age tier. No two children receive the same story.
Each story includes illustrated scenes generated to match your child's physical description — their hair color and style, eye color, skin tone, and gender. The character won't be a photograph of your child, but a warm, storybook-style illustration of a character that looks like them. Many parents tell us their children immediately recognize "themselves" in the images.
One new story every night — 365 per year. Each story is generated fresh and unique. Your child will never receive a repeat.
You choose the delivery time during setup — for example, 7:00 PM or 7:30 PM in your local timezone. The story arrives in your inbox at that time, ready to read at bedtime. You can update your delivery time from your account page at any time.

Ages & plans
We support children from birth through age 10, across four tiers:

Dreamlings (0–2): Short, rhythmic, soothing — ~75 words, 6 illustrated scenes.
Sprouts (3–5): Warm and playful — ~250 words, 5 scenes, 2–3 new vocabulary words.
Readers (6–8): Classic storybook arc — ~500 words, 4 scenes, richer vocabulary.
Explorers (9–10): Sophisticated narratives — ~800 words, 3 scenes, complex vocabulary introduced naturally.
